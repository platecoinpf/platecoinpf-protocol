# Platecoin Whitepaper

## 1. Vision and mission

**Vision.** A coin that is as easy to understand as the object it is named after. A plate is round, it is passed from hand to hand, and everyone can see what is on it.

**Mission.** Build a community token on Solana that keeps its books in the open: one public address, a market anyone can watch, and a rule that anything unconfirmed stays visibly blank instead of being filled in with promises.

Platecoin is a memecoin. It is not an investment product, it carries no promise of return, and nothing in this document is advice.

## 2. Principles

1. **Open books.** Every external fact lives in a single register record that is either stated, absent or unconfirmed.
2. **Nothing invented.** If a detail is not confirmed it is not shown. Inert records render without links or values.
3. **Context beside the coin.** The market board reads the wider Solana market live, independent of whether the coin's own address has been published.
4. **One address.** The contract address is shown whole, never shortened, in one place at the top of the site and on the official X account.

## 4. Roadmap

**Phase I: Set the plate.** Go live on Solana. The contract address appears in the top slot of the site and on the official X account.

**Phase II: Open the board.** The coin takes row one of the live market board, pinned above the trending tokens, with data drawn from DexScreener and pump.fun in parallel.

**Phase III: Fill the rack.** Grow the holder base and publish whatever has been confirmed. Anything not confirmed stays blank.

**Phase IV: Pass it around.** Community-led growth, and publication of the open-source repository once it is ready to be shown.
